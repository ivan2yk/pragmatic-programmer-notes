## Short summary
A few hungry soldiers arrive at a village where the villagers are poor or unwilling to share food. The soldiers say they can make a wonderful soup using only a stone.

Curious, the villagers gather around as the soldiers:
- put a stone in a pot of water and boil it,
- then casually say things like "it would be even better with a few carrots"
- or "a bit of meat would really bring out the flavor"

One bye one, villagers contribute small ingredients. In the end, everyone enjoys a rich, delicious soup, and the soldiers remove the stone.

## Core ideas
- Cooperation creates abundance, together people have more than they think
- The power of initiative

## Why it is often referenced today
- Software development: Starting a project with a minimal seed; start small, make progress visible, and invite contribution 
- Open source communities
- Team building and leadership
- Product development (MVPs)
- Change management: people contribute once they see momentum
