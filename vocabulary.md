# Vocabulary and Phrases

## 1. New Words
| Word | Meaning | Example Sentence |
|------|---------|------------------|
| **Undeterred** | Not discouraged or stopped by difficulties | Undeterred, the soldiers boiled a pot of water |

## 2. Phrases
| Phrase | Meaning | Example Sentence |
|------|---------|------------------|
| **They are stagnating on their job** | They are not moving forward or advancing on their job | |
| **Lame excuse** | A weak or unconvincing excuse, something that does not really justify what a person did or didn't |

## 3. Idioms
| Idiom | Meaning |
|-------|------------------|
| **Their spirits lifted** | They felt happier |
| **Break the ice** | Start a friendly conversation |
